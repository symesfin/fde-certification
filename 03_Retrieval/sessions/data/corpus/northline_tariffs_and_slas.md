# Northline Broadband — Tariffs, Terms & Service Levels

This page is the service desk's reference for what each tariff costs, what it commits to, and what the customer is owed when a commitment is missed. Figures are quoted exactly as published; agents must not round them or quote a tariff from memory.

## Pricing Philosophy

Northline prices on a fixed monthly charge per tariff with no introductory discount and no mid-term price rise. The trade is a minimum term: the faster the tariff, the longer the minimum term, in exchange for a lower monthly charge than the national operators list for the same speed. Early termination charges exist so that the minimum term means something, and are capped so that no customer pays more than the remaining term.

## Fibre 150 Home (NL-F150-12)

Fibre 150 Home is provisioned under product code `NL-F150-12`. The monthly charge is £29 per month, billed monthly in advance. The minimum term is 12 months. The tariff provides 150 Mbps download and 30 Mbps upload over full fibre, a Northline router on loan, and the residential support service level: a target time to restore of two working days, with no service credit.

## Fibre 500 Home (NL-F500-24)

Fibre 500 Home is provisioned under product code `NL-F500-24`. The monthly charge is £45 per month, billed monthly in advance. The minimum term is 24 months. The tariff provides 500 Mbps download and 75 Mbps upload over full fibre, a Northline router and one mesh unit on loan, and the residential support service level: a target time to restore of two working days, with no service credit. It is the most commonly sold residential tariff and the default upgrade offered by the save desk.

## Fibre 1000 Business (NL-F1000-36)

Fibre 1000 Business is provisioned under product code `NL-F1000-36`. The monthly charge is £85 per month, billed monthly in advance. The minimum term is 36 months. The tariff provides symmetric 1 Gbps full fibre, a static IP address, a business router on loan, and the business support service level: a committed time to restore of eight working hours with service credits as described below.

## Service Credits — Fibre 1000 Business

When a Fibre 1000 Business fault is not restored within eight working hours of the ticket being opened, the customer is credited one day's charge for each further working day or part day the fault remains open, up to the full monthly charge. Credits are applied automatically on ticket closure and do not need to be claimed. Faults caused by the customer's own equipment, by a planned maintenance window notified in advance, or by damage to the customer's premises are excluded.

## Service Targets — Residential Tariffs

Residential tariffs carry a target time to restore of two working days. The target is reported to the regulator but carries no service credit. The save desk may offer a goodwill credit of up to one month's charge where a residential fault has exceeded five working days, subject to the retention programme's rules.

## Early Termination — Residential

A residential customer who cancels inside the minimum term pays the remaining months at the monthly charge less a 20% early-termination discount, capped at twelve months' charge. A customer who moves to an address Northline cannot serve pays nothing. The router and mesh units must be returned within 30 days or are charged at £60 and £40 respectively.

## Early Termination — Business

A business customer who cancels inside the minimum term pays the remaining months at the monthly charge less a 10% early-termination discount, with no cap. The static IP address is released on the cancellation date.

## Payment Terms

Charges are billed monthly in advance by direct debit. A failed direct debit triggers a reminder after seven days and a service restriction (browsing to the account page only) after 21 days. Card payments taken over the phone follow the PCI DSS procedure in the glossary. The March billing incident, in which a cohort on the legacy fixed-wireless tariff was double-charged, was corrected by automatic refund and is the standing example of why agents must not take a second payment manually when a customer reports a failed one.

## Mobile Add-On

Mobile is sold only as an add-on to an active broadband tariff, at £10 per month per SIM with no minimum term beyond the broadband tariff's. It is resold on a national carrier's network, so mobile coverage faults are routed to the carrier through the NOC rather than diagnosed on the desk.

## Price Changes and Renewal

Northline does not raise the monthly charge during a minimum term. At the end of the term the tariff continues month to month at the same charge, and the customer may move to any current tariff without a new minimum term unless they take a new router.
